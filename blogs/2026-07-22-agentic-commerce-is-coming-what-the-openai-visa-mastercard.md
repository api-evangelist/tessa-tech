For years, AI could describe how to buy something. Now it is starting to do the buying. Agentic commerce, where an AI agent completes a real transaction on a person’s behalf, is moving from concept to live pilots, with names like OpenAI, Visa, Mastercard, and Stripe testing the plumbing that makes it work.
